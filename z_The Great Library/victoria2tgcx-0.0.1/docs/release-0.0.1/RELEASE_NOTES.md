First beta of **ATCU - Army Template and Conform Universal** for Victoria II 3.04 (single player).

- **Templates:** save army compositions and recruit them in one click.
- **Conform:** make an army in the field match a template. Extras are detached into a new stack, and the missing regiments are built and march to join it.
- **Universal:** the unit list, icons and build times come from the running game, so it works on vanilla and base mods.

Short checks passed on vanilla, HPM 0.4.5, Divergences of Darkness Fan Fork and TGC 2.5.2 (also with V2DLL 5.82). Other mods and multiplayer are untested. Only the 3.04 `v2game.exe` is supported, and ATCU turns itself off on any other build.

**Install:** download `ATCU-0.0.1.zip`, extract it and follow `README.txt`. You copy files only: no installer and no scripts.

Known limits: Conform stops tracking reinforcements after a save is loaded, and it never ships troops overseas. Continent and culture filters are planned.
