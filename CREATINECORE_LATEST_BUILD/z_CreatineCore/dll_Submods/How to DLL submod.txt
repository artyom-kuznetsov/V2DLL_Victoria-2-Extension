Whatever .dll you put here will be launched together along with creatinecore.
You can use this if you want to add some patches yourself.

DLLs also can be launched from a mod folder.
Quoting readme:
LOAD_DLL_FROM_MOD_FOLDER
CreatineCore will load any .dll from an active mod. In case you want to add new patches for your mod independently (made by maxioten).

How to get submods? Maybe you can find some on the internet.
How to make a submod? Well, if you have no idea about it - show AI the CreatineCore repo folder and tell it you want to do a patch of your own. :) (on your own risk)
